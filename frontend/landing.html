How it works

  1. 1

    Snap it

    Take a photo of a piece. The background comes off and it's sorted by type, color and style for you.

  2. 2

    It learns your closet

    Every piece knows what it goes with, when you last wore it, and whether it's in the wash.

  3. 3

    Today, it picks

    One outfit for today's weather and plans. Swipe for another, tap Wear this, and you're out the door.
